👩‍⚕️

Nurse Portal

Each nurse sees her own 10-patient monitoring list, patient vitals, priority, alerts, review actions, and monitoring updates.

Patient Portal

The patient experience is split into wearable connection and desktop app views, showing personal measurements, trends, alerts, and care-team status.
